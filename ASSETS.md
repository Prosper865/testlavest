## Logos and flags

- **Company logos:** rendered from the `simple-icons` package (CC0-1.0) in `features/market/logos.ts`, in each brand's colour. Microsoft uses a CSS-rendered four-square mark. Simple Icons has no Amazon or JPMorgan logos, so those companies are not listed.
- **Coin icons:** `public/logos/crypto/*.svg`, copied from the `cryptocurrency-icons` package (CC0-1.0).
- **Currency flags:** `public/flags/*.svg`, copied from the `flag-icons` package (MIT).
- `public/logos/tesla.svg` (from https://cdn.simpleicons.org) is still used on marketplace cards.

Brand marks identify market instruments and do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
